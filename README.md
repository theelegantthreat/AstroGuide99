Welcome to AstroGuide99! A comprehensive guide and resource hub for astronomy enthusiasts, beginners, and seasoned stargazers alike.

## About

AstroGuide99 is dedicated to providing accessible, accurate, and engaging content about astronomy, celestial observation, and space science. Whether you're looking to learn about constellations, understand planetary mechanics, or plan your next stargazing adventure, you'll find valuable resources here.

## Features

- Comprehensive astronomical guides and tutorials
- Stargazing tips and best practices
- Constellation maps and celestial event calendars
- Resources for both beginners and advanced enthusiasts
- Community-driven contributions and discussions

## Getting Started

To get started with AstroGuide99, check out our [documentation](./docs) and explore the various guides available in this repository.

### Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of astronomy concepts (helpful but not required)
- Interest in learning and sharing knowledge about space
